About Champasen

Our journey

From one mill to 12 export markets

2001

Where it all began

Champasen opens its first feed mill, supplying poultry farms in the local area.

2006

First export shipment

Our first trucks cross the border, starting a regional export business.

2012

New line and quality lab

A second production line doubles capacity, and every batch is now tested in-house.

2017

Livestock and aqua ranges

We launch feeds for cattle, pigs, fish and shrimp alongside our poultry range.

2021

10 export markets

Our logistics network reaches ten markets by road and sea.

Today

Growing with 3,000+ partners

500k tonnes of feed a year, delivered to 12 export markets, and still growing.
